Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Private SMS ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use it. The app is a personal SMS messaging application that can also act as your device's default SMS handler.

By installing or using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information We Access

The app requests certain Android permissions in order to function as a default SMS app. We access this data only to provide the app's features, and only on your device.

SMS and MMS messages

To send, receive, and display your text messages, the app reads and writes to your device's SMS/MMS storage (via Android's Telephony provider). Your messages are stored by Android itself, the same as with any SMS app you have used before — the app does not create a separate copy of your message content on any external server.

Contacts

The app reads your device Contacts to show sender names instead of raw phone numbers, and to let you pick recipients when composing messages or building message groups. Contacts data is read locally and is not uploaded anywhere by the app.

Phone state and call log (where the optional companion Phone app is installed)

If you choose to install the optional companion Phone app, it may request phone and call log permissions to display call history, handle incoming/outgoing calls, and support call-related features. This is entirely optional and separate from the core SMS app.

Microphone (only if you use call recording, companion Phone app)

If enabled in the optional companion Phone app and permitted on your device and under applicable law, call recording uses the microphone only during an active call you choose to record. We do not record calls automatically or without a clear on-screen indicator.

Notifications

The app posts notifications for incoming messages. You can control what is shown in these notifications from within the app's settings: you can turn off message previews, or set a notification privacy label that replaces the sender and message content. Notifications for locked conversations never show their content.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not upload the content of your SMS/MMS messages to any server we operate. Message text is only ever sent off your device if you turn on the optional AI Assistant, and then only to Google, as described in Section 4.
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
  • The app does not access your location.
  • We do not access another person's device or location through their phone number.

3. Locked / Private Conversations

The app includes an optional feature that lets you mark certain conversations as private, protected by a PIN or biometric unlock. This data (which conversations are private, your PIN, and related settings) is stored only on your device, is not transmitted anywhere, and is not accessible to us. If you forget your PIN or lose your device, we cannot recover this data.

4. AI Assistant (Optional, Off by Default)

The app includes an optional AI Assistant that can draft (and, only if you turn it on, automatically send) replies to incoming messages while you are away. It uses Google's Gemini API. The AI Assistant is switched off by default, and nothing described in this section happens unless you turn it on yourself in Settings → AI Assistant and enter your own Gemini API key.

When data is sent

Message content is sent to Google only when all of the following are true: the AI Assistant is switched on, Auto-reply is switched on, you are marked as away (manually or by your away schedule), and you have entered a Gemini API key. It is then sent once for each new incoming SMS. Messages in locked/private conversations, and messages from senders that are not phone numbers (such as service names and short codes), are never sent.

What is sent

  • The text of the new incoming message.
  • The text of up to the 10 most recent earlier messages in that same conversation (both received and sent), so the reply fits the conversation.
  • The customer type you assigned to that sender (for example, Wholesale or Retail), if any.
  • If you also turn on "Style learning": up to 8 examples of messages you have sent yourself, taken from your sent messages, so the reply matches your writing style.

The sender's phone number, their contact name, your contacts and your device identifiers are not sent as separate information. However, any names, phone numbers or other details that appear inside the message text are sent as part of that text.

Where it is sent

The data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection directly from your device to Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com), using the API key you provided. It is not sent to any server we operate, and we never see it. Google processes it under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • Gemini API Additional Terms of Service: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Important: if you use a free (unpaid) Gemini API key, Google's terms allow Google to use the content you send to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Do not turn on the AI Assistant for conversations containing information you do not want shared with Google in this way.

What stays on your device

Your Gemini API key is stored using Android's encrypted storage. Your product list, customer types and style-learning examples are stored only on your device. AI-generated replies are shown to you in a notification for review, unless you have turned on automatic sending, in which case they are sent as a normal SMS to the person who messaged you.

Turning it off

You can stop all data being sent to Google at any time by switching off the AI Assistant or Auto-reply, or by removing your API key, in Settings → AI Assistant.

5. Advertising

The app displays advertisements through Google AdMob. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be estimated), ad interaction data and diagnostic information, in order to serve, measure and protect ads. The app does not include your messages, contacts or phone numbers in ad requests. Google handles this data under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Ad consent

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ads are requested. You can review or change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Android settings.

Facebook (Meta) app measurement

To measure how many people install and open the app after seeing our ads on Facebook and Instagram, the app includes Meta's Facebook SDK (core only — no Facebook Login and no sharing). It starts only after you give consent where consent is required. It then sends Meta your device's advertising ID, an app-install identifier, basic device information (such as model, operating system version, language and time zone), your IP address, and events such as app installs, app opens and Google Play purchases. It never receives your messages, phone numbers, contacts or PIN. You can withdraw consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Meta handles this data under its own policy:

  • Meta Privacy Policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

You can stop ads entirely with the optional "Remove Ads" purchase described below. If you have removed ads, the Facebook SDK is not started either.

6. Subscriptions and Payments

The app offers optional "Remove Ads" purchases: a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase, all processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details — these are handled by Google Play. Your purchase is tied to your Google account and can be restored on any device signed into that account using the "Restore Purchase" option in the app.

7. Data Storage and Security

Sensitive settings (such as your PIN) are stored using Android's encrypted storage mechanisms. Your messages remain in Android's standard SMS storage on your device, protected by your device's own lock screen and security settings.

Message backups you create are saved as a file in the location you choose (or, for automatic backups, in the app's own storage on your device). They are not uploaded anywhere by the app and are not encrypted, so keep backup files somewhere safe. The app does not use Android's cloud backup, and it does not include any crash-reporting or analytics services other than the ad measurement described in Section 5.

Because standard SMS is not end-to-end encrypted at the network level, we recommend not sending highly sensitive information via SMS to recipients using other apps or carriers.

8. Your Choices and Controls

  • You can uninstall the app at any time, which removes all app-specific settings from your device.
  • You can revoke any permission (such as Contacts, Camera or Notifications) at any time from your device's Settings — some features will stop working if you do.
  • You can switch off the AI Assistant at any time (see Section 4).
  • You can change your ad consent choices from Settings → Ad privacy choices (see Section 5).
  • You can clear all of the app's data from your device's Settings → Apps → Private SMS → Storage.
  • You can block unwanted senders using the app's built-in blocking feature.

9. Data deletion

We do not run any server that stores your messages, contacts, PIN or other app data, so there is no account or copy on our side to delete. Here is how to delete each kind of data yourself:

  • PIN, private conversations and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them, as does Settings → Apps → Private SMS → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Text messages are kept in Android's shared SMS storage on your device, not inside the app, so uninstalling the app does not delete them. To delete messages, delete the conversations in the app (or in any SMS app on your phone) before or after uninstalling.
  • Backup files you export are saved where you choose (for example your Downloads folder). They stay there until you delete them yourself, for example with your phone's Files app. Automatic backups are kept in the app's own storage and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • AI Assistant: message text is sent to Google's Gemini API only when you have turned the AI Assistant on with your own API key (see Section 4). We never receive or store it. Turning the AI Assistant off, or removing your API key, stops any further sending; data already sent is held by Google under the Gemini API terms.
  • Advertising ID: you can delete your advertising ID in Android settings: on most phones Settings → Privacy (or Security & privacy → More privacy settings) → Ads → Delete advertising ID. Ad and measurement data already collected by Google AdMob and Meta is handled under their privacy policies (see Section 5), and you can withdraw ad consent in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
